The bundled campus.svg is a rendered map of an OpenStreetMap snapshot retrieved on 2026-11-26. Source extent (longitude, latitude, WGS84): 113.536,22.118 to 113.555,22.141. Original data are available at:
https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=113.536,22.118,113.555,22.141

The cartography projects geographic coordinates onto a local metre grid centred at 113.544° E, 22.129° N. It clips and simplifies the original land, water, road, path, and building geometry. The heatmap and interactive printer listings are separate application layers. The map is for campus orientation.
